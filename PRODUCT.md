# People

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Employees, managers, HR administrators and organization owners use the workforce sign-in. Technical Super Admin accounts use a separate sign-in.

## Product Purpose
People organizes attendance, leave, schedules and employee operations. ERP owns workforce master data and raw attendance.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Nuxt 4 and Nuxt UI frontend; FastAPI behind a cookie-based BFF. Preserve role boundaries, MFA enrollment and verification, authenticator-based password recovery, forced password changes, and server throttling feedback.

## Brand Commitments
People™ by Datlabs. Retain existing People and Datlabs symbols. Authentication surfaces use the approved editorial violet direction, responsive mobile-to-desktop layouts and adaptive light/dark appearance with a toggle. The user has also approved carrying the violet branding into a clean, compact platform shell, with the existing workspace and permission model preserved. The approved frontend scope now includes every existing page: compact dashboard summaries, responsive lists, clear/reset controls, pagination, recoverable request feedback and Nuxt UI dialogs. Preserve all business features and ERP read-only boundaries; these are presentation improvements, not new business capabilities.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing SVG symbols in app/web/public/branding and documented workflows in docs/system-architecture-and-flows.md. No customer proof, certification, legal URLs or support destinations have been supplied.

## Product Principles
Sign-in comes first. Distinguish workforce access from technical administration. Keep recovery and second-factor steps understandable. Never present invented workforce data or unsupported claims.
